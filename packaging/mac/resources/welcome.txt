This installs Scope, an oscilloscope and analyser plugin:

  - VST3 plugin   -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  - Audio Unit    -> /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  - Standalone    -> /Applications

After installing, restart your DAW (or rescan plugins) and look for "Scope"
under the effects from YourBrand.
